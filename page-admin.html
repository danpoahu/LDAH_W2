School Readiness Project

Edit

Supporting children in gaining the skills to be successful in their education through developmental screenings, family support, and parent education.

Edit

About the Program

Edit

The School Readiness Project (SRP) provides services for families of preschool aged children throughout O'ahu, Moloka'i, and Hawai'i Island. We provide developmental, social-emotional, hearing, vision, and autism screenings. SRP works to strengthen families through case management and parent education.

Edit

Our Partners

Edit

SRP is a collaborative project with: Aloha United Way, The Community Learning Center at Ma'ili, Harry & Jeanette Weinberg Kukui Center, and Kamehameha Schools.

Edit

Childhood Screenings

Edit
Edit

Vision Screening

Edit

We use the Plusoptix Digital Vision Screener to check your child's eyes. It is an accurate, non-invasive way to detect a variety of vision disorders in children.

Edit
Edit

Hearing Screening

Edit

Two methods can be used: Otoacoustic Emissions checks both the middle and inner ear, and Tympanometry checks for reduced eardrum movement caused by fluid, wax, or other problems.

Edit
Edit

Developmental Screening

Edit

Our qualified personnel use the Ages & Stages Questionnaire (ASQ-3) and ASQ-SE screening tools. Children with concerns are screened for autism using M-CHAT.

Edit

Our Services

Edit
Edit

Case Management

Edit

Case Management is provided for families of children identified with a specific need. Our staff will support you by explaining results, offering solutions, connecting you with resources, and following up on referrals.

Edit
Edit

Parent Workshops

Edit

Trainings are designed to increase understanding of health, developmental, and school readiness needs of preschool aged children. Learn what development should look like and review possible signs of delays.

Edit
Edit

Provider Services

Edit

If you are a provider and would like to add our screening services to your program or event, we can help! We offer vision, hearing, and developmental screenings, trainings, and resources.

Edit

Schedule a Screening

Edit

Contact LDAH to schedule a screening event in your community. Individual screenings are also available by appointment in both our Honolulu and Wai'anae offices.
