Dear Friend of Leadership in Disabilities & Achievement of Hawai‘i (LDAH),

For more than five decades, LDAH has been helping families navigate complex educational systems, access critical resources, and advocate for children with disabilities and developmental concerns. Every day, parents turn to LDAH for trusted guidance, training, case advocacy, and support during some of the most important decisions affecting their child’s future.

As a nonprofit organization, we rely on the generosity of individuals, families, businesses, and community partners to ensure these services remain available throughout Hawai‘i and the Pacific.

LDAH is revamping our membership program by offering parents, professionals, and community members an opportunity to stay connected with the Parent Center network of over 100 parent centers across the nation.

We invite you to become a member of LDAH and invest in stronger families, informed advocates, and brighter futures.

Become a Benefactor

Every membership includes

  • Local & national resources and materials from the Office of Special Education and Rehabilitative Services (OSERS)
  • Monthly local resource materials specific to IDEA and Hawai‘i Administrative Rules Chapter 60
  • Access to current training materials
  • Access to the LDAH Training Video Library — a full year of past Learning Labs and events, on demand
  • Downloadable slide decks from every Learning Lab
  • AI summaries of every Learning Lab training, with the key takeaways
  • Everything gathered in one private members-only portal
  • Our annual newsletter
  • Connection to the national network of 100+ parent centers
  • Friend level and beyond: eligibility for case advocacy services from our highly trained Special Education Consultants

Your membership makes a difference

  • Empower parents to become confident advocates for their children
  • Provide workshops, Learning Labs, and educational resources
  • Offer case advocacy and technical assistance to families navigating special education
  • Support early childhood screening and family follow-up services
  • Strengthen partnerships with schools, healthcare providers, and community organizations
  • Build future leaders who advocate for inclusive opportunities for all children
