Part 2 · Interactions

What counts as an interaction

A phone callThey rang you, or you rang them
A visitThe office, or out at a school
Email or webAn exchange about a child
A conversationA question answered at a table

These are what the funding reports count. An unlogged call did not happen, as far as the report is concerned.

Part 2 · Interactions

The button is always in the top bar

Wherever you are, + New Interaction is in the same place. Click it and the form opens on top of whatever you were doing.

Part 2 · The form

Mostly buttons. Very little typing.

Tap a channel, tap a tier, find the person, write a line or two, save. Nothing here can break anything.

Part 2 · The form

Interaction type — which tier of help

Most one-to-one help with a family is Tier 1. When you are genuinely unsure, ask before you tap — this is the field the funding is counted by.

Part 2 · The form

Who it was about, and which programme

Part 2 · The one rule

Type a few letters, then click the name

Notice it matches anywhere in the name — typing daniel found Daniel, Danielle and Nicholas-Daniel. Email and island are shown so you can tell two similar people apart.

Part 3 · One-off events

A gathering with no signup behind it

An outreach table, a clinic pop-up, a talk you were asked to give. It happened, so it needs to be counted — and you add it afterwards, from your own notes.

Part 3 · Getting there

Reports, then the pink button

Left menu → Reports → Event Attendance Report → + Add One-Off Event, top right.

Part 3 · The form

Four sections, top to bottom

1 · The event

Name, date, presenter, location, tier, description.

2 · The materials

What you packed and what you gave away.

3 · The people

Names if you have them, and the headcount.

4 · Save

And it is counted. You can reopen and edit it later.

Part 3 · Section 1

The name, and the date it happened

The date defaults to today. Change it to the day of the event, or your report lands in the wrong week.

Part 3 · Section 1

Presenter comes from the list

Pick the person who actually ran it. If the name is missing from the list, ask the office to add them rather than leaving it blank.

Part 3 · Section 1

Tier level tells the funders what this was

Location and description sit alongside it. That one sentence of description is worth thirty seconds — in a year it is the only thing telling anyone what this gathering was.

Part 3 · Section 2

Packed is what you brought. Disseminated is what you gave away.

Fill only the rows you actually used. Everything else stays at zero — you do not need to touch it.

Part 3 · Section 3

Search for the people you already know

Everyone you add here is marked as having attended. The + New contact button, in blue, is only for a family who is new to us.

Part 3 · Section 3

Adding a family who is new to us

Phone and email are optional. A name and a role is enough to get them on the record — the office can fill in the rest later.

Part 3 · Section 3

The headcount is enough on its own

Your best honest estimate beats leaving it empty. Names are not required — the number is what the report needs.

Part 3 · Section 4

Save, and it is counted

Saved events can be reopened and edited any time. Get it down while you remember it — you can tidy it up later.

Part 3 · Done

Your event, now on the report

The pink ONE-OFF tag marks it as one you added by hand. Click it to open and edit it again any time.
