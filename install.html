Step 1: Open the App First

Before you can install the app, you need to open it in your browser:

Launch LDAH App

Important: After clicking the button above and the app opens, follow the installation instructions below based on your device.

Then follow these instructions

Installation Instructions

1

Open in Safari

Make sure you're using the Safari browser (the compass icon). Other browsers won't work for installation.

2

Tap the Share Button

Look at the bottom of your screen and tap the share button:

Share button (square with arrow)

3

Find "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down in the menu and tap:

Add to Home Screen

4

Tap "Add"

You'll see the app name and icon. Tap the blue "Add" button in the top right.

Done! Check your home screen!
